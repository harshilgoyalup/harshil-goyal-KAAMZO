OUR PURPOSE

About Kaamzo

Connecting local skills with everyday opportunities across India.

Dignity of labour, transparency of pay, and real neighbourhood support.

In cities and towns across India, millions of skilled tradespeople—electricians, plumbers, masons, painters, cleaners—rely on word-of-mouth or informal street corners (chowks) to find daily work. Middlemen often extract heavy commissions, leaving workers underpaid and customers uncertain about quality.

Kaamzo is built to change that. We provide a clean, modern digital platform where local workers can showcase their genuine expertise, set their own fair rates, and connect directly with households and businesses that need them.

The Kaamzo Promise

  • ✓ Zero middleman cuts: Customers pay 100% of the agreed fee directly to the worker via Cash or UPI.
  • ✓ Transparent daily & hourly rates: No hidden surge pricing or artificial platform markups.
  • ✓ Neighbourhood discovery: Connect with verified professionals operating right in your locality.
